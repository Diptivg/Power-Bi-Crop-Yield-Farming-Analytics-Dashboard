# 🌾 Farming Crop Yield Analytics 

## Purpose
The **Smart Farming Yield Analytics System** is a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze crop yield patterns using sensor, soil, weather, and farming practice data. Its goal is to support data-driven decision-making for improving agricultural productivity and sustainability.

---

##  Tech Stack
- **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development  
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data storage and preprocessing  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Measures and calculated fields  

---

##  Data Source
The project uses a structured agriculture dataset containing smart farming and yield-related attributes.  
Key columns include:

- **Crop_Type** – Type of crop (Rice, Wheat, Maize, Soybean, Cotton)  
- **Fertilizer_Type** – Organic, Inorganic, or Mixed  
- **Region** – Farming region (South India, East Africa, Central USA, etc.)  
- **Soil_Moisture_%** – Soil moisture level  
- **Soil_pH** – Soil acidity/alkalinity  
- **Humidity_%** – Atmospheric humidity  
- **Rainfall_mm** – Rainfall amount  
- **Temperature_C** – Temperature in Celsius  
- **NDVI_Index** – Vegetation health index  
- **Irrigation_Type** – Drip, Manual, Sprinkler, or None  
- **Crop_Disease_Status** – None, Mild, Moderate, Severe  
- **Sowing_Month & Harvest_Month** – Crop cycle timing  
- **Yield_kg_per_hectare** – Crop yield measurement  

The dataset is used to evaluate how environmental, soil, and farming factors influence crop yield.

---

##  Features & Highlights
- **Sensor-Based KPIs**
  - Average Humidity, NDVI, Rainfall, Soil Moisture, Soil pH, and Temperature  

- **Crop & Fertilizer Analysis**
  - Average Yield by Crop Type  
  - Yield comparison across Organic, Inorganic, and Mixed fertilizers  

- **Disease Impact**
  - Yield variation by crop disease status (None, Mild, Moderate, Severe)  

- **Regional Performance**
  - Comparison of crop yield across multiple global regions  

- **Seasonal Insights**
  - Sowing Month-wise Yield trends  
  - Harvest Month-wise Yield patterns  

- **Irrigation Analysis**
  - Variance in yield across Drip, Manual, Sprinkler, and No irrigation  

- **Interactive Filtering**
  - Crop Type, Fertilizer Type, Region, Soil Moisture %, Sowing Month, Harvest Month, and Irrigation Type  

---

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **how climate, soil, farming practices, and timing affect agricultural yield**, making it suitable for **smart farming, research, and agri-policy decision support**.
